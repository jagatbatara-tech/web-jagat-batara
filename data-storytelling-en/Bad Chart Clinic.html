1. Diagnose

Tick every real flaw. Some options are decoys that sound plausible but are not problems; wrong ticks cost points, and ticking everything scores zero.

2. Choose the fix

Pick the redesign you would ship from four previews. One is best, others are partial or make things worse.

Answers lock. You then see Before → After, the reason for every flaw, and the source page.

How scoring works

Teaching heuristic, not an exam. Each case is worth 100 points: 60 for diagnosis (each real flaw spotted earns its share; each decoy ticked costs 1.5 shares; select-all scores zero) and 40 for the fix (best 40, partial 10 to 25, harmful 0). Your radar uses accuracy per skill axis, shown with the number of items behind it. Axes with fewer than four items are marked low confidence. Your level depends on the average score and on how even your axes are.
