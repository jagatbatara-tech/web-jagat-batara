Privacy and limits

Everything runs on your computer in this page. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved. Formula cells are read as the last value Excel saved, so a file that was never recalculated can show empty cells. Old .xls and .xlsb files, password-protected files and pivot-table reports are not supported; save the data as .xlsx or .csv first.

Columns

Confirm what each column is

The types below are guesses. Fix any that are wrong; the recommendations follow your choices.

Date = a point in time · Period = an ordered label such as Jan, Feb or a year · Number = something you add up or average · Yes/No flag = an event you count as a rate · Category = a group · ID / text = not used for grouping · Ignore.

Every recommendation comes from a short list of rules. You can see them all, and none of them look at your business context.

What it cannot do

  • It does not know your audience or decision, so edit the headline before using it.
  • It cannot tell whether a column was entered correctly or what a code means.
  • Headlines state facts about the numbers; they do not explain causes.
  • It does not fix pivot-table reports, several tables on one sheet, or a mix of units in one column.

Good habits

  • Check the preview and the column types every time; a confident chart on a misread table is still wrong.
  • Keep the notes under each chart with the chart when you share it.
  • When groups have few rows, treat their values with care.
